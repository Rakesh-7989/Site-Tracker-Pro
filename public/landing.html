— FAQ

Questions we hear often

Everything you need to know about how SiteTrack works for Indian builders.

How is "per org" different from "per user"?

Most SaaS charges ₹2,000 × every user you add. We charge ₹999 for the whole firm. Add 50 architects, 200 PMs, 500 contractors — same price. Scales with project count, not headcount.

Can my client see our internal data?

No. Postgres Row Level Security ensures the client role sees only: their project's milestones, released drawings, and invoices addressed to them. They cannot see BOQ, RA bills, labour register, or other clients.

Do you work offline at the site?

Yes — every write queues in IndexedDB when offline and syncs on reconnect. Photos, drawings, RFI updates all queue locally. Designed for 3G in basement excavations.

How does the RERA integration work?

You enter your project's RERA registration number once. We validate the format, store it on the project, and (where the state RERA API allows) push monthly progress reports automatically. Telangana + Karnataka first; others rolling out.

Is the data really mine?

Yes. Export any project as CSV, drawings as ZIP, RA bills as PDF — anytime. Custom plan customers can self-host. The 19 pure-function libs powering the app are open-source on GitHub.

What about WhatsApp Business API?

Business plan ships with native WhatsApp Business API — DPRs auto-send at 6PM, invoice links sent on creation. Basic/Pro plans use wa.me deep-links (still works, just one extra tap).
