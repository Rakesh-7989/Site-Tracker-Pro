# Loom Shoot Checklist — WhatsApp DPR 60-Sec
*Sprint 1, Day 2 · Session 30.2*

Print this. Carry it to the site. Tick every box in order. The Loom
is the warm-up asset the founder sends 48 hours before every Builder
meeting (see `docs/sales/DEMO_SCRIPT_DPR.md`). One day of shoot, one
re-cut budgeted. Don't over-engineer.

## Equipment list

### Phone 1 — The "supervisor" device (the talent's phone)
- [ ] Real Rs 8,000 Android. Suggested: **Realme C53** or **Redmi A2+**
      (both available at Lot Mobiles, Banjara Hills, ₹7,999 MRP).
      NOT iPhone — breaks Builder trust the moment it's spotted.
- [ ] WhatsApp Business installed + activated with a dedicated demo
      number (NOT the founder's personal number — the demo will land
      on the promoter side, not be the source). Use a fresh
      ₹99-prepaid SIM bought same day.
- [ ] Telugu keyboard enabled: Settings → Languages & Input → add
      `Telugu (India)` via Google GBoard. Verify by typing
      "నమస్కారం".
- [ ] Bhashini "Anuvaadini" app installed from Play Store (free,
      40MB) for voice-to-text fallback if WhatsApp's own voice
      transcription is patchy.
- [ ] Phone fully charged (100%) plus 10,000 mAh power bank in
      pocket. Basement parking lighting is hostile.
- [ ] Camera lens cleaned with microfibre — basement dust is a
      visual killer.
- [ ] Date + time set correctly (auto-network) — EXIF needs to
      match yesterday's project timeline reference.
- [ ] Location services ON for the WhatsApp app (Settings → App
      info → WhatsApp → Permissions → Location → While in use).

### Phone 2 — The "promoter" device (showing receive side)
- [ ] Mid-range Android or iPhone (a Redmi Note 12 / iPhone 13 looks
      believable for a Hyderabad builder promoter). NOT another Rs
      8,000 phone — promoter wouldn't use one.
- [ ] WhatsApp Business installed with a different number (founder's
      throwaway second SIM or a friend's number with permission).
- [ ] Save the "supervisor" demo number as **"Site Supervisor —
      Vasavi Vista"** in contacts so the chat header is
      believable.
- [ ] Notification preview ON so the message thumbnail is visible
      when it lands.
- [ ] Dark mode OFF — the cream/orange UI palette reads better in a
      basement-light scene.

### Camera C — The narrator B-roll device
- [ ] Founder's iPhone or DSLR. Records the split-screen B-roll +
      end-frame founder face-card piece (the part starting at 38
      sec in `DEMO_SCRIPT_DPR.md`).
- [ ] Tripod or gimbal. Handheld is fine if the founder has steady
      hands — but a wobble at second 40 looks unprofessional.
- [ ] Loom desktop app installed on the laptop that does the final
      cut (Mac / Windows / Linux all fine).

### Audio
- [ ] Lavalier (lapel) mic on the supervisor talent (₹400 boya BY-M1
      is enough; clip to collar of construction shirt; cable into
      Phone 1's 3.5mm or USB-C — verify the demo phone has a port
      OR carry a ₹150 OTG adapter).
- [ ] Phone 1 audio test: record 5 seconds of room tone in the
      basement parking BEFORE the talent arrives. If you hear AC
      hum or generator noise > 50 dB, scout a quieter pillar.
- [ ] Founder voiceover for end-card: studio-quiet room (apartment
      cupboard with hanging clothes is a passable booth). Record
      separately, layer in post.

### Lighting
- [ ] Basement parking is dim (50–100 lux typical). Carry one
      battery-powered LED panel (Godox SL60W or any ₹2,500
      equivalent). Position 45° off-camera, just below face line so
      shadow falls UP not down (down-shadows = villain lighting).
- [ ] No on-phone flash. The "harsh white square" look kills
      authenticity.
- [ ] Avoid daylight bleed from basement ramp — too high contrast
      between mouth of ramp and pillar-shot location.

### Tripod / handheld
- [ ] Two cheap phone tripods (₹500 each, Lot Mobiles or Amazon
      same-day) for Phone 1 (supervisor) and Phone 2 (promoter
      receive) shots.
- [ ] Phone clamps fit a phone in vertical orientation (9:16) for
      both.

### Misc
- [ ] Black gaffer tape (1 inch) — cover any backed Builder /
      project signage that's not yours (privacy + IP).
- [ ] Spare microfibre cloth.
- [ ] Bottled water (basement parking dust + 30-min hold = thirsty
      talent).
- [ ] Tally sheet (this doc on paper, plus a single A4 for
      "lessons learned during shoot").

## Talent

### Site supervisor talent
- [ ] First choice: **a real Telugu-speaking site supervisor**.
      Borrow from a friend's project for 90 minutes. Pay ₹500 cash
      + lunch. Authenticity is everything.
- [ ] Fallback: a Telugu-speaking acquaintance willing to look the
      part. Wear: faded blue/orange polo, dark trousers, dusty
      safety shoes, hard hat (white). NOT a fresh polo — pre-rub
      with chalk dust.
- [ ] Brief the talent: "You will speak 3 Telugu sentences from the
      phrase bank, take 1 photo of [reference object], and tap
      send. We'll do 3 takes. ₹500 in your hand at the end. Cool?"
- [ ] Get a 1-line consent on video before recording: "[Name],
      [Date], I consent to my voice and face being used in
      SiteTrack Pro's marketing." Keep this clip safe.

### Promoter / receive-side person
- [ ] Can be the founder, or any well-dressed friend. Only hand and
      phone screen are in frame — full face NOT visible. Saves a
      consent step.

### Founder
- [ ] End-frame face card. Shave / trim that morning. Plain dark
      polo (no logo). The CTA needs trust — don't overdress, don't
      underdress.

## Editing notes

### Loom-side
- [ ] Cut tight — every second is currency. 60 sec cap is non-
      negotiable.
- [ ] Hard-coded English captions for every Telugu line. Use the
      phrase bank file as the caption source so they match
      verbatim.
- [ ] Captions: 28pt Inter Bold, white, with semi-transparent black
      box behind. Position lower-third.
- [ ] No background music. The basement room tone IS the music.
- [ ] One subtle whoosh sound effect at the 22-sec mark when the
      WhatsApp message lands on Phone 2 (optional, only if it
      doesn't fight the lav-mic dialogue).
- [ ] End-card: 5 seconds, founder face mid-frame, pricing card
      bottom-right, WhatsApp number bottom-centre. Hold until the
      Loom auto-end-card.

### Loom export
- [ ] Render: 1080×1920 vertical (9:16). MP4, H.264, 8 Mbps.
- [ ] Loom URL is the primary asset — host the MP4 in
      `public/demos/` only for the marketing site embed fallback.
- [ ] Track Loom analytics: who watched, how much, where they
      dropped off. Iterate v1.0.2 if the 60-sec floor watches less
      than 50% of frames.

## Contingencies

### "The site supervisor backed out at 6:30am"
- [ ] Plan B: founder shoots the supervisor side themselves in
      Telugu. Wear the dusty polo + hard hat. Don't show full
      face. Authentic enough for Day 2; perfect can wait for Day
      45.

### "The basement parking is locked"
- [ ] Plan B: stairwell on a known-friend's under-construction
      project. Founder's network has 3+ such projects in Banjara
      Hills / Gachibowli at any time.
- [ ] Plan C: re-shoot at a different basement next morning. Budget
      slip 1 day.

### "Phone 1 won't transcribe Telugu voice cleanly"
- [ ] Plan B: send the voice note WITHOUT live transcription. The
      Loom can show the voice-note message landing + the supervisor
      saying the Telugu sentence aloud as voiceover. Transcription
      can be added in post via Loom's auto-captions.

### "The shoot ran over budget — Loom is 75 sec, not 60"
- [ ] Cut beat 32–38 (BuildNow badge segment). Move that promise to
      the end-card text. Honest, faster.

### "Founder's voice is sick / hoarse on shoot day"
- [ ] Reschedule. Don't ship a wheezy voiceover. The founder face
      card is the trust moment.
