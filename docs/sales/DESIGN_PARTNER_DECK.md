# Design Partner Deck — Slide outline
*Sprint 1, Day 1 · Session 30.2*

This is the markdown source for the design-partner pitch deck. Use it
to drive the .pptx export in `docs/pitch/`. Length cap: **9 slides,
12-minute walkthrough max**. Founder presents in person at the
Builder's office.

Three rules:

1. Every slide has ONE proposition. No bullet-soup.
2. No claim survives without a verified source — every "Powerplay
   has / doesn't have" claim must trace to
   `docs/research/VERIFIED_GAPS_MATRIX.md`.
3. Pricing slides cite per-org annual numbers only. Per-user-per-month
   framing is retired (see `docs/business/POSITIONING.md`).

## Slide-by-slide founder notes

| # | Time budget | Founder tone | Critical Builder cue |
|---|-------------|--------------|---------------------|
| 1 | 30 sec | Calm, slow | Eye contact, builder reads firm name |
| 2 | 60 sec | Confident | Builder nods at "Hyderabad-first" |
| 3 | 90 sec | Curious | Builder volunteers what they use today |
| 4 | 90 sec | Factual | Builder reacts to the case-study screenshot |
| 5 | 90 sec | Validating | Builder asks "what's BuildNow?" → explain quickly |
| 6 | 120 sec | Honest | Builder asks "what else is hidden?" → say "16 stubs, you can verify" |
| 7 | 60 sec | Direct | Builder picks tier with finger |
| 8 | 90 sec | Specific | Builder asks about exclusivity micro-segment definition |
| 9 | 60 sec | Closing | Builder gives a YES / MAYBE / NO + scheduled next step |

**Total budget**: ~12 minutes presentation + 5 minutes Builder
questions + 3 minutes commitment = 20-minute meeting.

If the meeting runs to 30 minutes, the Builder is bought. If it ends at
15, capture the NO reason verbatim — that's the data we need.
