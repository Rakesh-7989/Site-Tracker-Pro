# Loom Storyboard — WhatsApp DPR 60-Sec
*Sprint 1, Day 2 · Session 30.2*

A 12-shot storyboard mapping the 60-sec Loom beat-sheet to actual
camera framings, on-screen text, transitions, and timing budget.
Print this. Carry it. Cross off each shot as it's captured.

Source of beats: `docs/sales/DEMO_SCRIPT_DPR.md`. Source of voice
lines: `docs/sales/TELUGU_PHRASE_BANK_DPR.md`. Equipment + logistics:
`docs/sales/LOOM_SHOOT_CHECKLIST.md`.

## Per-shot specification

### Shot 1 — Wide establishing (0–3 sec)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Duration | 3 sec |
| Framing | Wide shot, vertical 9:16. Basement parking entry / ramp mouth in mid-ground. Supervisor walking IN from ramp toward camera. |
| Camera | Static tripod at ground level, slight up-angle (looks "real worker entering"). Camera C (founder's iPhone). |
| Subject | Supervisor in safety hat + dusty polo, lav mic clipped under collar (hide cable). |
| Audio | Room tone only — basement echo + distant labour murmur. NO dialogue yet. |
| Captions | None. |
| Transition out | Hard cut to Shot 2 (no fade). |

### Shot 2 — Medium shot: hand + phone (3–7 sec)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Duration | 4 sec |
| Framing | Medium shot of supervisor's torso + face + Rs 8,000 Android in hand. Phone screen lit, WhatsApp Business open at chat with "Promoter — Vasavi Vista" |
| Camera | Handheld at chest height, slight movement (≈2° sway) to feel real-not-staged. |
| Subject | Supervisor looks at phone, taps the microphone icon. |
| Audio | Room tone. Soft tap-sound on screen. |
| Captions | None. |
| Transition out | Hard cut to Shot 3 (audio continues). |

### Shot 3 — Medium close-up: voice note recording (7–14 sec)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Duration | 7 sec |
| Framing | MCU on supervisor's face + upper torso + phone held to mouth. Mouth visible (caption sync). Hard hat partial in frame top edge. |
| Camera | Static tripod at supervisor face height, 5 ft distance. Phone 1 lens or Camera C — whichever has cleaner audio. |
| Subject | Supervisor speaks Beat 2 line: "Naynu ee-roju Vasavi Vista basement parking lo unna, slab pour ayindi." |
| Audio | Lav mic primary. Phone 1 mic secondary. Clean Telugu. |
| Captions | "I'm at Vasavi Vista basement parking today, the slab has been poured." Lower third, white Inter Bold 28pt + semi-transparent black bg. Highlight "Vasavi Vista" in safety-orange. |
| Transition out | Hard cut to Shot 4 (audio continues into shot 4). |

### Shot 4 — Close-up: phone screen showing photo capture (14–20 sec)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Duration | 6 sec |
| Framing | CU of phone screen filling 80% of vertical frame. Photo capture UI visible. Supervisor's thumb in shot. Behind phone (out of focus): basement pillar + chalked column-line. |
| Camera | Phone 2 (or Camera C macro) close to Phone 1, captures the screen + finger interaction. |
| Subject | Supervisor speaks Beat 3 line over the visuals: "Aaj eighty cubic metres concrete vesinaru. Photo teesi geotag tho pampisthunna." |
| Audio | Continued lav-mic voice + soft photo-shutter SFX when thumb taps. |
| Captions | "Today eighty cubic metres of concrete were poured. I'm sending a photo with geotag." Highlight "eighty" in safety-orange. |
| Transition out | Hard cut to Shot 5 (no fade, no swipe — momentum matters). |

### Shot 5 — Close-up: send tap (20–23 sec)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Duration | 3 sec |
| Framing | Tight CU on the WhatsApp send arrow + supervisor's thumb tapping it. Phone fills 60% of frame, slight angle. |
| Camera | Camera C macro. |
| Subject | Supervisor speaks Beat 4 line: "Send press cheystunna." Then taps. |
| Audio | Voice + a single, satisfying "swoosh" SFX as the message lifts off (the WhatsApp native sound is fine). |
| Captions | "Pressing send." |
| Transition out | Whip-pan to split-screen (Shot 6) — 0.3 sec motion blur from supervisor side to promoter side. |

### Shot 6 — Split screen begin: message in flight (23–25 sec)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Duration | 2 sec |
| Framing | Vertical split — supervisor's phone left half (still on the sent screen), promoter's phone right half (notification dropping in from top). |
| Camera | Static composite in post — Phone 1 footage (recorded earlier) + Phone 2 footage (recorded after). |
| Subject | The message animation: bubble appearing in promoter's WhatsApp. |
| Audio | Subtle WhatsApp notification ding (built-in WhatsApp sound, not custom). |
| Captions | None — let the visual carry. |
| Transition out | Cross-fade (0.3 sec) to Shot 7 (promoter side only). |

### Shot 7 — Promoter phone receives (25–32 sec)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Duration | 7 sec |
| Framing | MCU of promoter's phone screen + hand. Coffee mug in foreground (dressing the "7am" beat). Background: blurred kitchen / dining table. |
| Camera | Phone 2 footage; static handheld. |
| Subject | Promoter's hand taps the message → it opens → voice note + photo + transcribed text + geotag visible. |

| Transition out | Hard cut to Shot 8 (BuildNow badge highlight). |

## Editing pass

| Pass | What | Time budget |
|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Rough cut — assemble shots in order, verify 60 sec | 30 min |
| 2 | Audio mix — lav mic + VO levels matched (-12 dB peak), no clipping | 15 min |
| 3 | Captions — verbatim from phrase bank, English-only | 20 min |
| 4 | Colour grade — pillar-shot may need lift in shadows; cream end-card needs warmth (+5 yellow) | 15 min |
| 5 | Final QA — play at 0.5× speed, check no Builder firm signage visible, no dialogue overlap, captions sync | 10 min |
| 6 | Render + Loom upload | 10 min |

**Total post-production**: ~1h 40min budget. Don't perfectionism.
