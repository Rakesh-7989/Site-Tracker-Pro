# Warm-Intro Mapping — M1–M5 Hyderabad Builders
*Sprint 1, Day 3–5 · Session 30.2*

The founder's network atlas — every plausible warm-intro path to the
5 named Hyderabad mid-size builders (My Home, Aparna, Sumadhura,
Vasavi, Lansum). Print this. Carry it. Highlight the paths to walk
this week. Update as paths open or close.

Cold DM lands ~5–15% of the time. Warm intro lands ~40–60%. Sprint
1's go/no-go bar (≥ 1 PILOT-YES or ≥ 2 MAYBE) effectively requires
at least 3 of these targets to be reached via warm intro.

Pair with `docs/sales/LINKEDIN_TARGET_LIST.md` (target profiles +
verified company facts) and `docs/sales/LINKEDIN_OUTREACH_SEQUENCE.md`
(messaging templates after the intro lands).

## How to use this file

1. Day 3 morning — founder reads through. Highlights 3 highest-
   probability paths.
2. Day 3 afternoon — founder reaches out to the 3 intro nodes
   directly (WhatsApp / call / coffee invitation).
3. Day 4–7 — intros are made on the founder's behalf to M1–M5.
4. Day 7 onward — founder follows up with the actual builder
   target.

## Network nodes the founder needs to activate

### Node 1 — CREDAI Telangana

**What it is**: Confederation of Real Estate Developers' Associations
of India, Telangana chapter. Apex builder lobby + monthly meets +
shared procurement initiatives.

**Why it matters**: Single highest-leverage node. Three of M1-M5
(My Home, Aparna, Sumadhura) have promoter/leadership presence here.

**Founder's move**:
- Find next monthly meet date — call CREDAI Telangana office Mon AM.
- Pay membership fee if required (₹50,000-100,000 typical for builder
  membership; ₹5,000-10,000 for tech-vendor associate membership —
  founder can join as a tech vendor / construction-SaaS startup).
- Attend with business cards + Loom QR poster.
- Goal: identify 2 promoters / GMs at M1–M5 willing to take a
  follow-up call.

**Cost**: ~₹10,000 fee + 1 day. **ROI**: 3+ warm intros to M1–M5
decision-makers.

### Node 2 — Architect firms (mid-size)

**What it is**: Hyderabad has ~15 mid-size architect firms that
work repeatedly with M1–M5.

**Why it matters**: Architects are deeply embedded with builders'
GM Projects + design teams. Architects also benefit from a good
DPR tool (their site supervisor uses the same tool).

**Founder's move**:
- Identify top 5 architect firms by mapping M1–M5 project pages
  (typically credits an architect).
- LinkedIn DM founder of each: "Free 90-min consultation in
  exchange for 15-min feedback on a tool I'm building."
- 1 of 5 will say yes → that's the warm intro to a builder.

**Cost**: 1 day of pro-bono consulting. **ROI**: 1+ builder warm
intro per architect.

### Node 3 — Civil / sub-contractor + material vendor network

**What it is**: RMC plant managers (UltraTech, ACC, JSW), steel
rolling mill local managers, sub-contractors floating across 3-5
builder firms.

**Why it matters**: They know which builder needs what TODAY. They
also know which builder pays on time / has procurement budget free.

**Founder's move**:
- Identify 3 RMC plant managers + 2 steel rolling mills in
  Hyderabad outskirts (Patancheru, Mahbubnagar, Medak).
- Coffee meeting at their location with the Loom on a tablet.
- Direct ask: "Which 2 builders should I demo this to?"

**Cost**: 3 days of coffee meetings. **ROI**: Highly specific +
verified intros.

### Node 4 — Family-office / social

**What it is**: Banjara Hills residential community + Hyderabad
business-family circles.

**Why it matters**: Promoters' families know each other socially.
Trust travels through these channels.

**Founder's move**:
- Identify 3 friends / family-of-family in the Banjara Hills /
  Jubilee Hills resident networks.
- Soft ask at a social event: "I'm building a thing for Hyderabad
  builders. Who do I know who knows someone at My Home / Aparna /
  Sumadhura?"

**Cost**: 0 — leverage existing relationships. **ROI**: Variable;
depends on founder's existing network density.

### Node 5 — ISO consultants + CAs

**What it is**: Compliance-side experts. Hyderabad has ~5 ISO
consultants and ~10 CA firms specialising in builder filings.

**Why it matters**: They know which builder is RERA-stressed / which
needs e-invoice help / which would value the BuildNow integration.

**Founder's move**:
- LinkedIn search + DM to top 3 ISO consultants — pitch as
  compliance-first product.
- Reach out to 2 CA firms that file RERA-TG quarterlies for
  multiple builders.

**Cost**: 1 day. **ROI**: Specific compliance-pain warm intros to
B2 (Aparna) primarily.

## Honest failure modes

1. **CREDAI Telangana doesn't accept tech-vendor associate
   membership** → fallback to attending their next public seminar
   / industry event as an attendee (~₹2,000 ticket).
2. **Lansum founder doesn't reply** → fallback to LinkedIn employee
   evangelism path (Node 1.5).
3. **Vasavi walk-in receptionist sends founder away** → leave 1-page
   summary + Loom QR + business card; follow up via LinkedIn
   afterwards. The walk-in attempt itself signals seriousness.
4. **All marquee paths fail** → fall back to M6–M13 alternates in
   `docs/sales/MEETING_LOG_2026-06.md`. Reset positioning to mid-tier
   builder ICP, may need 2nd Sprint 1 extension week.

## Don't do this

- ❌ Pretend to know someone you don't.
- ❌ Ask CREDAI Telangana for builder phone numbers directly — they
  protect promoter privacy. Approach via attending meets, not
  through their directory.
- ❌ Cold-DM Dr. Rao (My Home) directly. He won't reply. Use the
  GM-Projects path.
- ❌ Send the Loom + pricing in the FIRST cold message via warm
  intro — let the warm intro do the framing first.
- ❌ Burn the warm intro by being pushy with the actual builder
  meeting. If they say MAYBE, accept it.
