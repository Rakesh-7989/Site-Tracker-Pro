# 60-Second Loom — WhatsApp Daily Progress Report
*Sprint 1, Day 1 · Session 30.2*

A single 60-second screen recording the founder sends as the warm-up
asset BEFORE every Builder meeting. Captures the wedge in one minute.
Lives at `public/demos/whatsapp_dpr_60s.mp4` once recorded.

## What it shows

A site supervisor on a basement parking site holds a Rs 8,000 Android,
records a Telugu voice note, takes a photo with geotag, hits send. On
the right half of the screen, the promoter's WhatsApp lights up at the
same moment with the transcribed report, the photo, and a "Verified by
BuildNow Telangana" badge.

That's it. No dashboard. No login. No app install. Just WhatsApp.

## Format

- **Length**: 60 seconds. Cap. If it runs to 75, recut.
- **Aspect**: 9:16 vertical (WhatsApp + Loom landscape both work).
- **Audio**: Founder voiceover in Telugu-transliterated English. No
  background music.
- **Captions**: Hard-coded English subtitles (Builder may forward to
  English-speaking promoter or English-speaking site engineer).
- **End frame**: Founder face card with WhatsApp number + "DM me to
  lock your pilot slot · INR 29,999/yr first 5 only".

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Founder shoots on an actual Rs 8,000 Android (Realme C-series or
      similar). NOT iPhone — that breaks the relatability.
- [ ] Real basement parking site, real Telugu voice, real WhatsApp
      account. NOT a mocked screen recording.
- [ ] Geotag shows Hyderabad coordinates (lat ~17.4°, lon ~78.4°).
- [ ] BuildNow badge — if the real integration isn't shipped yet
      (Sprint 2), show a HONEST placeholder badge that reads
      "BuildNow integration — Sprint 2". Do not fake it.
- [ ] End-frame WhatsApp number is the founder's actual number.
- [ ] Captions are accurate verbatim — no embellishment.
- [ ] Loom (not raw mp4) — Loom auto-captions for ESL viewers and
      tracks who watched how much.

## Where to send it

- **Pre-meeting**: Send Loom link 48 hours before the in-person
  meeting via WhatsApp. Builder watches before you arrive; founder
  shows up to discuss, not present.
- **Cold reach**: Lead the LinkedIn / WhatsApp cold message with the
  Loom + a single question: "Aapke site supervisor isko use karenge?
  YES / NO / TELL ME MORE."
- **Post-meeting follow-up**: If Builder says "let me think" — resend
  the Loom 7 days later with the message: "Bhai, kuch sochne ka final
  call?"

## What this Loom is NOT

- ❌ Not a "comprehensive product demo". 60 seconds. One workflow.
- ❌ Not a feature tour. Save features for the in-person meeting.
- ❌ Not promotional. No music swells, no testimonials, no logos.
- ❌ Not a deepfake. Real founder, real site, real Telugu.
