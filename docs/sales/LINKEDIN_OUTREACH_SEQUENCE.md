# LinkedIn Outreach Sequence — Sprint 1 Day 4–7
*Sprint 1, Day 3–7 · Session 30.2*

The DM templates the founder uses to reach Group A (ex-Powerplay /
current Powerplay users) and Group B (Hyderabad builder
decision-makers) on LinkedIn. Each template is short, specific, and
ends with a single binary ask.

Pair this doc with `docs/sales/LINKEDIN_TARGET_LIST.md` (who to reach
out to) and `docs/sales/DEMO_SCRIPT_DPR.md` (the Loom asset). Pricing
+ positioning anchors per `docs/business/POSITIONING.md` + `docs/business/PRICING.md`.

## Three rules

1. **One question per DM.** Never two. Builders skim.
2. **Loom in DM #2, not DM #1.** Group A DM #1 = curiosity-led
   question; Group B DM #1 = Loom-led pitch. The asymmetry matters.
3. **Stop after 3 touches.** If no reply after T1 → T2 → T3 over 14
   days, move on to the next target.

## Group A — Question-led (ex-Powerplay / current Powerplay users)

These targets don't need to be sold. They need to be invited to
share what they know. The DM is a research request, not a pitch.

### A-T1 — First touch (Day 4)

```
Hi [Name],

I'm Rakesh — Hyderabad-based founder building SiteTrack Pro, a
construction record-keeper for Hyderabad builders.

Saw you [worked at Powerplay / use Powerplay at [Firm]]. I'm trying
to learn how Powerplay actually works in the field BEFORE I waste 90
days building things you already have.

Would you be open to a 15-min call this/next week? No pitch, just 5
questions about your daily workflow. I'll share what I learn from
across all interviews back with you.

Cool?

— Rakesh
[founder WhatsApp number]
```

**Constraints**:
- Max 90 words.
- No mention of pricing, blockchain, RERA, or AI. Save those for the
  call.
- The "I'll share what I learn back" is the reciprocity hook —
  honour it.

### A-T2 — 7 days later if no reply

```
Hi [Name], following up in case my last note got buried.

15 min, this/next week, 5 questions. Whichever day works for you —
I'll move my calendar.

Or — if you don't have time, even a 5-line WhatsApp reply on this
one Q would be gold: when you file/help-file RERA TG quarterly
progress, do you do it through Powerplay or through the state portal
directly?

Either way thanks 🙏
— Rakesh
```

**Constraints**:
- Mention the time-asymmetry ("I'll move my calendar").
- Offer the lowest-friction substitute (a 5-line text answer).
- Pick the SINGLE most valuable Q for them (RERA for PMs, GSTN for
  CFOs, voice for site supervisors).

### A-T3 — 7 days later if still no reply (final)

```
Hi [Name], last note from me — promise.

If now is not the right time, all good. If you change your mind in
the next few months, my WhatsApp is +91 ____.

Wishing you well at [Firm].
— Rakesh
```

**Constraints**:
- Set the boundary explicitly ("last note").
- No ask.
- Leave the door open without a deadline.

## Group B — Loom-led (Hyderabad builder decision-makers)

These targets need to be shown, not told. The Loom does the work.

### B-T1 — First touch (Day 4)

```
Namaste [Name] sir/ma'am — Rakesh Boyapati. Hyderabad-based founder.

Recorded a 60-sec Loom for [Firm] — SiteTrack Pro is a WhatsApp +
Telugu daily-progress tool for Hyderabad mid-size builders, anchored
to BuildNow Telangana. Powerplay anchor — 30% less, per-organization
pricing, not per-seat.

Loom: [URL]

First five Hyderabad builders get a design-partner deal at INR 29,999/yr
(24-month lock + 3-month logo exclusivity in your micro-segment).

15-min meeting next week — your office, your time. Cool?

— Rakesh
[founder WhatsApp number]
```

**Constraints**:
- Lead with the Loom — that's the differentiator vs other cold
  outreach.
- Mention the pilot anchor only AFTER the Loom URL.
- The binary ask = the 15-min meeting.
- NO mention of features beyond "WhatsApp + Telugu + BuildNow". The
  Loom shows the rest.

### B-T2 — 5 days later if no reply

```
Namaste sir/ma'am — following up.

If the Loom wasn't useful, what specifically would have made it more
relevant for [Firm]? Even one-line feedback shapes our product.

If it WAS useful but the timing is wrong — when's better? I'll come
to your office [Banjara Hills / Gachibowli / Kondapur / your area]
whenever works.

— Rakesh
```

**Constraints**:
- Make the no-reply a chance for feedback (not pressure).
- Confirm physical-presence willingness (founder is in Hyderabad).
- Offer flexibility on time.

### B-T3 — 7 days later if still no reply (final)

```
Namaste sir/ma'am — last note from me.

Pilot slots are filling up — 4 more to lock by mid-July. If [Firm]
wants in, the door is open. If not, no hard feelings.

Reach me on WhatsApp +91 ____ anytime.

— Rakesh
```

**Constraints**:
- Soft scarcity ("4 more slots") — honest, since the cap is 5 total
  pilots.
- "No hard feelings" — keep the relationship for later.
- Leave WhatsApp as the lowest-friction channel.

## Reply handling

### "Yes, let's talk" / "Send me available slots"

Reply within 1 hour with **3 specific slots** in the next 7 days, NOT
"any time works". Lead with the slot the founder most wants
(usually a morning slot, builder pre-work).

```
Excellent — three options:
1. Wed 3 June, 10:30 AM
2. Fri 5 June, 9:00 AM
3. Mon 8 June, 4:00 PM

I'll come to your office (or Zoom — your call). 15 min.

Reply with the number and I'll confirm.
```

### "Maybe later" / "Send me a one-pager"

```
Sure — attaching a 1-page summary [link to docs/sales/DESIGN_PARTNER_DECK.md
or the v1 deck PDF].

If "maybe later" turns into "yes" any time in next 30 days, the
pilot slot is still open. After that I'll have signed 5 builders
and the design-partner price moves to standard ₹49,999/yr.

— Rakesh
```

Don't push. The one-pager + soft deadline does the work.

### "Not interested" / "We have Powerplay"

```
Got it — appreciate the honest reply.

One curiosity Q if you'll indulge me: what's the ONE thing about
Powerplay you wish was different? I won't pitch in response — I'm
gathering feedback across builders to shape what we build.

If too busy, no worries. Wishing you well.
— Rakesh
```

This converts a NO into a research interview. ~30% conversion to
useful feedback in founder's experience.

### "Send pricing" (without showing interest)

```
Sure — Pro tier ₹49,999/yr per org (not per user — add as many site
supervisors as you want), Business ₹89,999/yr. Pilot ₹29,999/yr if
you're one of the first 5.

Full breakdown: [docs/business/PRICING.md or pricing page on sitetrackpro.in].

Want to talk through what's right for [Firm]? 15 min next week.
```

Quote in writing immediately. Don't be coy. Then re-pitch the
15-min ask.

### "What is this BuildNow integration?"

Reply with the Loom URL + this paragraph:

```
BuildNow Telangana (buildnow.telangana.gov.in) is the state's
unified approval portal launched Feb 2025. It uses blockchain
audit trails + WhatsApp status updates as core features — exactly
the architecture we built. So when your firm's audit log gets
asked about by a RERA inspector or banker, it ties back to the
same standard the state government chose.

Easier to show than tell — 15 min next week?
```

If reply rate floor missed → rewrite template, A/B test with next
batch. Founder is allowed to iterate the template — do NOT keep
sending a broken cold-DM.

## Common founder mistakes to avoid

- **Don't mention "AI", "blockchain", or "21 features" in the DM.**
  All retired per Sprint 1 freeze.
- **Don't ask for two things.** One question per DM.
- **Don't apologize for asking.** ("Sorry to bother you" reads as
  low-status — bad signal to a builder.)
- **Don't promise specific Sprint 2 features that aren't shipped.**
  Honesty is the wedge per `docs/business/POSITIONING.md`.
- **Don't follow up daily.** 5-day cadence between T1 → T2, 7-day
  between T2 → T3. Builders find daily nag annoying.
- **Don't ghost.** If a reply arrives, founder responds within 1
  hour during business day.
