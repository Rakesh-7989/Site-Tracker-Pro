### LinkedIn search recipe

Founder runs these queries IN SEQUENCE in LinkedIn's people search:

1. **`"getpowerplay" OR "Powerplay construction" Bangalore`** — surfaces
   ex-Powerplay employees in Bengaluru (Powerplay HQ).
2. **`"Powerplay" "site engineer" OR "project manager" Hyderabad`** —
   surfaces builder-side staff currently using Powerplay in Hyderabad.
3. **`Accel Powerplay`** — Accel's Series A note (verified source)
   identifies the lead; LinkedIn surfaces ex-Powerplay folks who left
   for adjacent firms.
4. **2nd-degree LinkedIn**: Founder's existing network mapped via
   "Mutual connections" filter on each verified ex-Powerplay profile.
5. **Open Source Intelligence**: Tracxn / Crunchbase pages for
   Powerplay's leadership team lists Co-founders — search them on
   LinkedIn + check their "Followers" tab for ex-employees engaging.

## Founder's outreach playbook by tier

### Tier 1 — Marquee (My Home, Aparna)

- NEVER cold-DM the founder/promoter directly.
- ALWAYS go through warm intro (CREDAI / architect / family-office /
  ISO consultant for Aparna).
- LinkedIn outreach to GM/Head/VP-level is acceptable IF the Loom is
  attached and the message uses the verified pricing comparison
  (per `docs/business/POSITIONING.md`).
- Founder physically attending CREDAI Hyderabad monthly meet is the
  highest-leverage move for these targets.

### Tier 2 — Established mid-size (Sumadhura, Vasavi)

- LinkedIn cold DM to COO / Head Construction / Director is acceptable.
- Warm intro via subcontractor / architect preferred but not mandatory.
- For Vasavi specifically: founder physical drop-in at Banjara Hills
  office is viable (founder is local resident).

### Tier 3 — Accessible mid-size (Lansum)

- LinkedIn cold DM to promoter is acceptable.
- Smaller firms with 5/5 culture = direct founder-to-founder DM lands
  most often.

### Group A — Ex-Powerplay / users

- LinkedIn 1st-degree DM (founder-to-stranger) is acceptable IF the
  ask is genuinely 15 min and not a sales pitch.
- Use the question-led template (`docs/sales/LINKEDIN_OUTREACH_SEQUENCE.md`).
- Offer a Loom AFTER scheduling, not before. Group A doesn't need
  to be convinced — they need to be invited to share knowledge.
