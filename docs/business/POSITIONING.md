## The one-line pitch

> SiteTrack Pro — the Hyderabad-first construction record-keeper that
> runs over WhatsApp in Telugu, anchored to BuildNow Telangana.

That's it. No "AI-powered". No "blockchain-enabled". No "enterprise
construction management platform". Five proper nouns: Hyderabad,
WhatsApp, Telugu, BuildNow, Telangana.

## Who it's for

**ICP (in priority order)**:

1. **Hyderabad mid-size builder** (RERA-registered, 5–50 active
   projects, INR 100–500 cr annual turnover). Examples: My Home,
   Aparna, Sumadhura, Vasavi, Lansum, Trendset, Hallmark, Vamsiram,
   Anuhar, Rajapushpa. **Powerplay has zero case studies featuring
   these names** (verified 3-0).
2. **Hyderabad small builder / contractor** (1–5 active projects).
   Lower-priority because pricing + retention economics are weaker.
3. NOT YET: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Chennai. Sprint 5+ at
   earliest, after 2 Hyderabad case studies are published.

## Who it's NOT for

- Procore-tier global builders (₹100+ cr ARR target — wrong wedge).
- Builders already deep on Powerplay procurement-credit (Mar 2026
  launch — switching cost is too high; ignore for 12 months).
- States without a BuildNow-equivalent govt portal (Sprint 6+).
- Non-construction adjacent verticals.

## Pricing — the corrected version

The deep-research workflow verified Powerplay's actual pricing is
**per-organization annual, not per-seat monthly**. The
`archive/marketing/index.html` claim of "Procore INR 31k/user/mo vs SiteTrack
INR 999/org/mo" was anchored to a wrong Powerplay assumption.
Corrected anchors below.

| Tier | SiteTrack Pro v3 (locked in Sprint 1) | Powerplay (verified Jun 2026) | Positioning |
|------|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------|-------------|
| **Pilot (design partner)** | **INR 29,999/yr** · first 5 builders only · 24-month lock · 3-month logo exclusivity in micro-segment | n/a | "Partnership, not discount." |
| **Pro** | **INR 49,999/yr per org** · ≤25 users · ≤10 projects · BuildNow sync · Telugu+English · WhatsApp DPR · founder onboarding | INR 71,999/yr · 15–20 users | **30% under** Powerplay anchor, NOT 70% under |
| **Business** | **INR 89,999/yr per org** · ≤60 users · unlimited projects · multi-state RERA (when shipped) · GSTN e-invoice · blockchain handover · Hyderabad CSM · quarterly on-site | INR 1,19,999/yr · 30 users | **25% under** Powerplay anchor |
| **Enterprise** | **INR 2,49,999+/yr** starting · custom users · white-label promoter app · API · on-prem audit log mirror · named sales engineer | Custom quote | Premium, not commodity |

**Rules**:

- Never quote a price under **INR 49,999/yr** to a non-pilot.
- The INR 999/mo and INR 12,000/yr Basic tier from
  `docs/business/PRICING.md` is **retired in Sprint 1**. Replaced by the
  pilot tier above.
- All per-user comparisons in marketing copy are retired. Speak in
  per-org annual numbers, because that's what Powerplay actually
  charges.
- The INR 29,999/yr pilot price requires 24-month commit + co-creation
  agreement (see `docs/business/PILOT_AGREEMENT_v1.md`). It is not a public
  discount.

## The five proof points (Sprint 1 messaging)

1. **BuildNow Telangana is built on the same approach.** (verified —
   buildnow.telangana.gov.in uses blockchain + WhatsApp). If a state
   government picked this architecture, your firm's audit trail is in
   good company.
2. **Powerplay has zero Hyderabad marquee case studies.** (verified
   3-0). Be one of our first five and own that wall.
3. **Per-organization pricing, not per-seat.** Add your site
   supervisor without paying extra. (Powerplay charges per-seat.)
4. **Telugu voice DPR, not translated string tables.** Your
   supervisor speaks Telugu into WhatsApp. The promoter gets a
   transcribed report at 7am. Not yet sure if Powerplay can do this
   — verifying via Sprint 1 interviews.
5. **Founder-led on-site activation.** 90 minutes at your office in
   Banjara Hills / Gachibowli / Kondapur. Not a webinar. Not a Loom
   video. The founder shows up.
